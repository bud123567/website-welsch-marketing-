# Welsch Marketing design

## Purpose

A marketing site for businesses looking for a custom website or paid advertising. The primary action is completing the existing Typeform inquiry.

## Direction

Bright, spacious, practical. Retain the red brand cue, with a cool paper background, charcoal typography, and a prominent two-column inquiry panel immediately below the introduction. The portfolio uses real site previews; Corner’s is the featured project. Avoid glass overlays, animated cursors, unsupported outcome claims, and hidden content.

## Tokens and typography

`styles.css` owns the runtime tokens: paper #f6f7f8, white #ffffff, ink #20242c, muted #58616c, red #b82636, line #d9dfe4. Space Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for body text, with Arial fallbacks. Maximum content width 1240px. Cards use restrained 6–12px corners.

## Behavior and accessibility

Native portfolio links open in a new tab, announced by the portfolio introduction. Mobile navigation supports Escape and outside-click dismissal. Visible focus, skip link, reduced motion, and a single-column mobile layout are required. The embedded Typeform retains live ID `01KBZTCJP9MBPVX948Y415XC35`; its resolved form is `wFckcTv0`. The direct fallback must point to the same form. Typeform owns question styling, validation, and submission.
